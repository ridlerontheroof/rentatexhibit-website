# Woods Crossing homepage design comps — direction notes

Three homepage comps for Woods Crossing (850 N. Hwy 89, North Salt Lake, UT — Class B, 1 & 2 bed,
mountain bench, minutes from downtown SLC). All use the real bundle content/photos in
`_shared/content.ts`. The winning direction becomes the visual spec for the new site project.
These same notes appear as sticky notes beneath each comp frame on the canvas.

## 1. Wasatch Warm — outdoorsy (`WasatchWarm.tsx`)
- **Palette:** warm sand `#F4EFE5`, deep forest `#2B362B`, rust orange `#C65932`
- **Type:** Fraunces (headings) + DM Sans (body)
- **Why it fits:** leads with the property's strongest asset — the mountain-bench setting. Earthy,
  golden-hour tones read honest and comfortable, not luxury.
- **Tradeoffs:** most "branded" of the three; leans lifestyle over information density.
- **Pick if:** the brand should feel rooted in the Wasatch outdoors.

## 2. Clean Contemporary (`CleanContemporary.tsx`)
- **Palette:** warm ivory `#FAF9F5`, deep sage `#416152`, pine `#25372A`
- **Type:** Outfit (headings) + DM Sans (body)
- **Why it fits:** airy, uncluttered, easy to scan — "a freshly cleaned apartment with the windows
  open." Sage keeps it warm and natural instead of stark.
- **Tradeoffs:** safest direction; least differentiated from other well-done modern apartment sites.
- **Pick if:** the brand should feel calm, trustworthy, and current.

## 3. Bold & Friendly (`BoldFriendly.tsx`)
- **Palette:** slate navy `#1C2B4A`, golden yellow `#FAC61A`, cream `#FAF8F5`, coral `#F46746`
- **Type:** Bricolage Grotesque (headings) + DM Sans (body)
- **Why it fits:** confident color and rounded shapes give it real personality — energetic and
  neighborly, never intimidating or pretentious.
- **Tradeoffs:** strongest personality, so also the most polarizing; yellow/coral must be used with
  discipline on inner pages.
- **Pick if:** the brand should stand out from beige competitor sites and feel welcoming to families.

## Shared constraints
- Class B tone: warm, approachable, value-forward, trustworthy — never black-marble luxury, never
  the dated 2012 template look.
- Facts (rents, availability, pet policy) come from the Aug 6, 2026 scrape and must be confirmed
  before launch — see the bundle's `docs/MISSING_AND_UNCERTAIN_FACTS.md`.
