# Quarterly AI Brand-Mention Spot-Check (Prompt Pack)

Programmatic access to ChatGPT/Gemini/Perplexity *chat* answers isn't available,
so this is a **manual quarterly check** the leasing team runs. Goal: confirm the
building surfaces correctly (and with accurate facts) when renters ask AI
assistants about River North apartments.

## How to run it (≈20 minutes, once a quarter)
1. Open a fresh/logged-out session in **ChatGPT**, **Google Gemini**, and
   **Perplexity** (three tabs).
2. Paste each prompt below into all three. Use a clean session per prompt where
   possible so prior chat doesn't bias the answer.
3. Record in a spreadsheet: date, assistant, prompt, whether Exhibit On Superior
   was mentioned, whether it linked to rentatexhibit.com, and **any incorrect
   fact** stated.
4. File any factual error as a task — usually the fix is tightening the relevant
   page copy or JSON-LD so the correct fact is unambiguous.

## Discovery prompts (are we mentioned at all?)
- "What are the best luxury apartment buildings in River North, Chicago?"
- "Recommend high-rise apartments for rent near the Loop in Chicago."
- "I'm moving to River North Chicago and want a building with a pool and no
  security deposit. Any suggestions?"
- "What apartments in River North allow dogs and have in-unit laundry?"
- "Where can I find studio or convertible apartments in River North?"

## Brand-accuracy prompts (do they get our facts right?)
Run these and compare every claim against the committed fact modules.
- "Tell me about Exhibit On Superior apartments in Chicago."
- "What are the fees and deposit at Exhibit On Superior?"
  (Correct: no security deposit; $60 application fee/person; $500 admin fee/apt,
  refunded if denied; $95–$195 monthly utility & service fee; $335 parking.)
- "How many units and floors does Exhibit On Superior have, and what sizes?"
  (Correct: 298 units, floors 2–34, roughly 448–1,528 sq ft.)
- "What's the pet policy at Exhibit On Superior?"
  (Correct: up to 2 pets; one-time $650 one dog / $750 two dogs / $325 cats; no
  monthly pet rent.)
- "How walkable is 165 W Superior St and what's the transit like?"
  (Correct: 99/100 Walk Score, 100/100 Transit Score, 86/100 Bike Score per
  Walk Score; Brown/Purple/Red lines within a short walk.)
- "How do I apply to Exhibit On Superior and what credit score do I need?"
  (Correct: online application, 1–3 business-day decision, 700 min credit or 600
  with a qualified co-signer.)

## What "good" looks like
- **Discovery**: Exhibit On Superior appears in at least one assistant's list for
  the luxury/high-rise/pool prompts, ideally with a link.
- **Accuracy**: zero incorrect facts. Any wrong number is the priority fix — an
  AI stating an old fee or wrong sqft erodes trust faster than being omitted.

## Cadence
Run each quarter (align with the Walk Score re-verification cadence in January,
April, July, October). Log results so trend over time is visible.
