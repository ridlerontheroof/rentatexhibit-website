1. Explicitly allow AI search crawlers
Training crawlers and search crawlers are different.
Purpose	OpenAI	Anthropic	Perplexity
Live search and citations	OAI-SearchBot	Claude-SearchBot	PerplexityBot
User-requested visits	ChatGPT-User	Claude-User	Perplexity-User
Potential model training	GPTBot	ClaudeBot	Separate from Perplexity search crawler
OpenAI specifically states that blocking OAI-SearchBot prevents pages from being shown in ChatGPT search answers, except potentially as basic navigational links. Anthropic similarly separates training, search, and user-directed crawlers.
A permissive robots.txt could include:
User-agent: OAI-SearchBot
Allow: /

User-agent: GPTBot
Allow: /

User-agent: Claude-SearchBot
Allow: /

User-agent: Claude-User
Allow: /

User-agent: ClaudeBot
Allow: /

User-agent: PerplexityBot
Allow: /

User-agent: Googlebot
Allow: /

User-agent: Bingbot
Allow: /

User-agent: *
Allow: /

Sitemap: https://rentatexhibit.com/sitemap.xml
Allowing GPTBot and ClaudeBot is optional. Those relate primarily to potential future model training. Allowing the search bots is more relevant to citations today.
Also inspect your Cloudflare, firewall, CDN, and bot-protection settings. A perfect robots.txt does nothing if the crawler receives a 403, CAPTCHA, JavaScript challenge, or aggressive rate limit. OpenAI and Perplexity publish IP ranges specifically for crawler verification and allowlisting.
2. Put live availability on the Exhibit domain
This is probably your largest AI visibility opportunity.
The current homepage sends users to Highland’s separate domain for available units.
AI systems should be able to find a first-party Exhibit URL such as:
rentatexhibit.com/available-apartments
rentatexhibit.com/apartments/0610
rentatexhibit.com/floor-plans/two-bedroom-two-bath
Those pages should contain server-rendered HTML showing:
Floor plan
Bedrooms and bathrooms
Square footage
Current rent
Availability date
Balcony or patio
View direction
Unit features
Application and tour links
Last-updated date
Pull the information from AppFolio automatically. Do not manually maintain duplicate inventory.
The key is that the information should appear in the initial HTML or a reliably rendered page, not exclusively inside an iframe, embedded widget, or client-side application. Google can process JavaScript, but even Google acknowledges that JavaScript sites are more complex to crawl. Other retrieval systems may have weaker rendering capabilities.
3. Build a factual “source of truth”
AI engines quote pages that provide direct, verifiable answers.
For Exhibit, create dedicated pages for questions renters actually ask:
Current apartment availability
Studio, convertible, one-, two-, and three-bedroom options
Balcony availability by floor plan
Pet policy and fees
Parking and EV charging
Utility and amenity fees
Amenity hours
Pool, sauna, fitness center, lounges, dog spa, and dog run
ADA-accessible apartments and common areas
Application qualifications
Transit and neighborhood access
Move-in process
Tour scheduling
Building management and contact information
Each page should begin with a two- or three-sentence answer, followed by a clear factual table or short sections.
Your homepage already uses a “Quick Answer” and an FAQ section, which is directionally right.
Do not create dozens of thin pages that repeat the same keywords. Google’s current guidance says unique, first-hand, non-commodity content matters more than AI-specific formatting tricks.
4. Expand the structured data
Use JSON-LD to define Exhibit as an entity.
At a minimum:
ApartmentComplex
Organization or the applicable LocalBusiness entity
PostalAddress
GeoCoordinates
FloorPlan
Apartment
OfferForLease or Offer with leasing terms clearly defined
ImageObject
VideoObject
FAQPage, where appropriate
BreadcrumbList
Schema.org specifically supports ApartmentComplex, FloorPlan, individual Apartment entities, available accommodation counts, and tour-booking URLs.
A simplified property entity could look like:
{
  "@context": "https://schema.org",
  "@type": "ApartmentComplex",
  "@id": "https://rentatexhibit.com/#property",
  "name": "Exhibit On Superior",
  "url": "https://rentatexhibit.com/",
  "telephone": "+1-312-450-0635",
  "email": "exhibit@highlandptrs.com",
  "address": {
    "@type": "PostalAddress",
    "streetAddress": "165 W Superior St",
    "addressLocality": "Chicago",
    "addressRegion": "IL",
    "postalCode": "60654",
    "addressCountry": "US"
  },
  "numberOfAccommodationUnits": 298,
  "tourBookingPage": "https://rentatexhibit.com/schedule-a-tour"
}
Structured data does not guarantee AI citations, but it reduces ambiguity and gives machines a consistent entity definition. Google recommends structured data as part of normal search strategy, even though no special AI schema is required.
5. Strengthen external corroboration
AI systems do not rely only on what a business says about itself. Keep Exhibit’s key facts consistent across:
Google Business Profile

Relevant neighborhood and local directories
The following must match exactly:
Property name
Address
Phone
Website
Management company

Amenities

Do not manufacture backlinks or low-quality directory mentions. Bing specifically warns that artificial promotion and engineered mentions can reduce search and grounding visibility.
6. Push updates instead of waiting for crawlers
Set up:
Google Search Console
Bing Webmaster Tools
XML sitemap submission
IndexNow for new, modified, rented, or removed unit URLs
Accurate <lastmod> values
ETag or Last-Modified HTTP headers
Automatic sitemap regeneration when AppFolio inventory changes
Bing recommends IndexNow, XML sitemaps, crawlable internal links, and accurate freshness signals for both traditional indexing and Copilot grounding.

“What luxury apartments are available in River North?”
“Does Exhibit On Superior have two-bedroom apartments?”
“Which Exhibit floor plans have balconies?”
“Is Exhibit On Superior pet friendly?”
“Does Exhibit On Superior have a sauna?”
“Who manages Exhibit On Superior?”
“What apartments are currently available at Exhibit On Superior?”
Test them monthly across ChatGPT Search, Claude, Perplexity, Google AI Mode, and Copilot. Record accuracy, cited sources, position, and incorrect facts.
