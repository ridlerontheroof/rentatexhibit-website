* How much does it cost to live at Exhibit On Superior?
* What fees are required in addition to rent?
* Are utilities included?
* Does Exhibit offer move-in specials?
* How much is parking?

Apartments

* Does Exhibit On Superior have convertible apartments?

* Which units have balconies?
* What views are available?

Amenities

Location

Trust and Citation Signals

Answer engines prefer facts that can be corroborated across independent sources.

Exhibit has some third-party presence, including Apartments.com, Birdeye, Facebook, YouTube, Zillow/Trulia-related results, and Google reviews.  

The problem is consistency. At least one third-party listing describes the property as offering studios through two bedrooms, while the website says studios through three bedrooms.  

That inconsistency can reduce confidence.

Directory cleanup checklist

Audit the following fields across every platform:

* Property name
* Address
* Phone number
* Email
* Office hours
* Bedroom range
* Square-footage range
* Pet policy
* Amenities
* Management company
* Website URL
* Current photos
* Tour link
* Availability link

The phone number deserves immediate attention because an older Facebook result shows a different number from the current website.
