Pet Policy

Sienna Chicago is a pet-friendly community! We welcome Cats & Dogs with some breed restrictions and a 2 pet limit per unit. A one-time non-refundable pet fee per pet is required, as well as a monthly pet rent per pet. Please contact the office for more details.

Amenities

From Sienna Apartments, you can walk to the nearby University of Chicago Gleacher Center, Loyola University Downtown Campus, Northwestern University Feinberg School of Medicine, and Northwestern University Pritzker School of Law.

Michigan Avenue is the main thoroughfare for bus access in the area.  Walk two blocks west from Sienna Apartments and find CTA bus routes: 2, 3, 26, 143, 146, 147, 148, 151, 151, and 157.  The CTA Grand/State CTA Red Line station is a few blocks west on the other side of Michigan Ave.
