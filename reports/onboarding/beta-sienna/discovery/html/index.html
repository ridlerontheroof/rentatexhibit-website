Nestled within Chicago’s Streeterville neighborhood, Sienna offers a limited number of boutique rental apartments. Here, 50 private flats combine higher ceilings with spacious floor plans to create an environment that balances an upmarket but free-flowing vibe. Fewer units reduce the commotion common to high-rise living while offering convenient day-to-day access without delay. Sienna also provides easy access to Lake Michigan, the Magnificent Mile, and more. If you want a unique approach to living, our apartments will fit you and your style.
