# Avoid Rental Scams

Source URL: https://www.woodscrossingslc.com/rental-scams

Last source scrape: August 6, 2026

The Woods Crossing source site warns that scammers may use the property name or property manager name without authorization. Renters should verify listings through official channels, meet or confirm legitimate ownership and management, avoid suspicious payment requests, sign a written lease, and report suspected fraud.

## Safety points from the source page

- Be cautious of listings that seem too good to be true.
- Compare listings with official property and manager websites.
- Avoid cash, wire, Venmo, Zelle, PayPal, or similar payment requests before a verified application or lease.
- Do not pay money before signing a written lease, except documented application payments tied to an actual application.
- Use reputable management companies and official communication channels.
- Treat personal social-media profiles requesting money as a warning sign.
- Report suspected fraud to local law enforcement or the Federal Trade Commission.
