# Woods Crossing Virtual Leasing

Source URL: https://www.woodscrossingslc.com/virtual-leasing

Last source scrape: August 6, 2026

The source virtual-leasing page lists five steps: choose a floor plan, fill out an online application, schedule a move-in day, e-sign the lease, and pick up keys.

The source page says application processing may include identification, paystubs or bank statements, references, rental history, background check, credit check, and social security number.
